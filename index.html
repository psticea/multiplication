Menu & Parent Guide

The game is paused. Here’s how it works and what your child is learning.

How to play

  • Press and hold a coloured number square — it becomes a corner of your rectangle.
  • Drag across the grid. The square under your finger is the opposite corner.
  • The rectangle turns green when its area matches the number, blue when it is still too small and red when it is too big or blocked.
  • Let go on a green rectangle to clear that number. Clear all 5 to win!

The learning idea

  • Multiplication is shown as area: rows × columns = total squares.
  • Children discover that a number like 12 can be 2×6, 3×4 or 6×2 — the same product with different shapes (factor pairs & commutativity).
  • Counting the grid squares links the picture to the number sentence shown while dragging: “3 × 4 = 12”.
  • Because a cleared rectangle vanishes, children plan an order: clear small numbers first to make room for bigger ones — early strategic thinking.
